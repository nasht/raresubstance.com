Subs Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Subs is an iPhone app that helps junior soccer coaches give every player equal time on the field. It is designed to work without collecting any information about the person using it or the players they coach.

Data we collect: None. Subs does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage analytics, identifiers, or location. It makes no network connections.

Teams and player names: The teams, player names and game formats you enter are saved only on your iPhone, inside the app. Games in progress are not saved at all. None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications: If you allow notifications, Subs schedules reminders on your iPhone for when a sub is due. They are created and delivered on the device; no notification service or server is involved.

Accounts, ads and purchases: There are no accounts or sign-in, no advertising, and no in-app purchases.

Third-party services: None. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code.

Children: Subs is used by adults. Coaches may enter children's first names to tell players apart; those names stay on the coach's iPhone and are never collected by us.

Changes: If this policy changes, we will update this page. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact: Questions about this policy? Reach us at privacy@raresubstance.com.
