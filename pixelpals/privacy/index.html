Pixel Pals Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Pixel Pals is a pixel-art drawing app for children on iPad. It is designed to work without collecting any information about the person using it.

Data we collect: None. Pixel Pals does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage analytics, identifiers, or location. It makes no network connections.

Drawings: Drawings, their names, and which ones are hearted are saved only on the iPad, inside the app. The app's settings, such as whether fingers or Apple Pencil draw, are saved on the iPad too. None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Accounts, ads and purchases: There are no accounts or sign-in, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no links that leave the app.

Third-party services: None. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code.

Children: Pixel Pals is made for children. Because it collects no information at all, it collects none from children.

Changes: If this policy changes, we will update this page. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact: Questions about this policy? Reach us at privacy@raresubstance.com.
